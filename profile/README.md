## AI SAFE EARTH

AI SAFE EARTH was born as a response to digital imbalance, under one critical assumption: as digital systems grow more intelligent, that imbalance amplifies disruptive social risks. We have a direct action approach and we believe the community layer is the appropriate one to respond to this humanity challenge.

---

## The problem we refuse to ignore

A digital intermediate layer is thickening between human connections, and the models and agents that manage those connections become more powerful every day. When a single layer mediates how people talk, decide and relate, whoever controls it gains outsized power, and communities lose agency.

The risk is not a distant superintelligence. It has already begun: the slow erosion of human agency and the concentration of power are a nowadays reality.

Some forecasts put transformative AI in years, not decades. We may be unprepared for the effects, and we cannot wait for perfect research or perfect policy. So we treat social infrastructure the way we treat a power grid: as critical infrastructure to be protected and strengthened, urgently, before digital imbalance and advanced AI combine into something we cannot undo.

## BUILD

This is the action arm. We build safe apps that rebalance digital and physical social interaction and reinforce local communities and social dynamics. Physical and local networks are the safety net. Social infrastructure is critical infrastructure, and we build to make it stronger, fast, before AGI, malicious actors using AI and digital imbalance create a critical combination.

### Applications for digital balance:

#### AI against attention economy.
We apply AI to turn the algorithmic loop into a bridge toward real life. We don't use AI as an addictive form of interaction. We use it to bring people back to people or back to real-world interactions, pulling users out of infinite scrolling and dopamine drain and into face-to-face socialising and public life.

The research is published and evolving as a [living book](https://ai-safe-earth.github.io/AI-Safe-Territory/).

## COMMUNICATE

 #### ... to turn evidence into pressure and awareness. 
 This branch creates the materials for AI safety education, in many kinds of programmes and formats. It gives research a punchy shape and supports any action with communication. Using data, real examples and red-teaming scenarios, we build critical mass and move decision-makers to act. We find the gaps between AI risks and people's awareness of them, and close them with effective AI safety literacy.
